# Citation policy

- A citation must use the `timestamp_url` returned by the search command.
- Do not strip the `t=` timestamp parameter.
- Put the citation immediately after the claim it supports.
- A source can support only what appears in its retrieved passage.
- Label interpretation as synthesis, not as a direct Hormozi statement.
- Prefer multiple independent video passages for high-stakes or broad recommendations.
- Captions may contain transcription errors. Preserve uncertainty when wording is unclear.
- This tool provides educational source retrieval, not legal, financial, medical, or individualized professional advice.
